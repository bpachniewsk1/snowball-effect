What is stored on your device

Your progress (rolls, streak, unlocked snowmen, nickname, settings) is kept in your browser's local storage so the game works. It never leaves your device unless anonymous statistics are on (below).

Anonymous statistics

To improve the game (which answers people try, how hard categories are), the game sends gameplay events to our database: a random player ID created in your browser, your nickname and snowman, the answers you give with their tier and how long you took, wrong guesses, finished rolls, unlocks, rough device type, language, screen size, the referring site's domain, and your country (from the network, not GPS).

We do not store your IP address. It is turned into a one-way hash mixed with a secret that changes every day, so a visit can be counted as unique for that day but cannot be traced back to you or linked across days.

Data is stored with Supabase (database hosting) in the EU. It is kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running and improving the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

About & credits

Snowball Effect is a non-commercial fan game inspired by the daily rarity format of Krillion and the look of Undertale. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to either game or their creators; all names belong to their owners.

The music, sound effects, snowmen and other art are original to this game. Fonts: Press Start 2P (CodeMan38) and DotGothic16 (Fontworks), both under the SIL Open Font License. Answer lists use data from Wikidata (CC0) and Wikipedia page-view counts.
